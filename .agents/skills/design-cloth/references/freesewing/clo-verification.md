# CLO transfer and fit

1. Check CLO availability and confirm a live read with the CLO 3D bridge `clo_status` and `scene_info` or the corresponding installed integration. Protect the open project before replacing its scene. Use native Save Project to checkpoint working edits and verify the checkpoint includes the garment and avatar. On this bridge, scene_export of a ZPRJ can produce an avatar-only file even with garment pieces visible in the open project. Use a separate project, and preserve and restore the original session if reusing the live app.
2. Transfer a single verified FreeSewing draft via garment DXF or the CLO pattern API. Export from structured paths where possible. Select the intended sewing contours, map closures, notches and internal lines explicitly, handle mirrored pieces and cut-on-fold, and account for the SVG and CLO Y-axis conventions. Preserve curves or subdivide to a checked tolerance. Compare mm dimensions and the seam or perimeter lengths before and after transfer and inspect the CLO 2D layout. Document tolerances or representation differences.
3. Pick a measurement-matched avatar and pose. Record its actual relevant measurements and their source along with the fabric preset or measured fabric. Arrange the editable pieces around the body, construct their seams and closures from the source assembly, and verify directions, counts and side/orientation. Test fasteners with CLO's native tools when possible. For a snap proxy, write down what it represents and which snap behavior it cannot validate. For a single-panel bib, keep the panel intact and place the two ends at the back neck before closing. A proxy avatar or a temporary edge stitch supports a limited simulation trial.
4. Run CLO simulation, inspect front, side and back (including close-ups of key seams or closure), and confirm the garment stays in place with plausible coverage and no visible clipping, twist or unstable drift. Use CLO fit, strain or stress maps where they answer the actual fit question. Save the project and inspect it again after reopening. For active or adjustable designs, test relevant poses or settings as requested, recording each case.
5. If the fit fails, distinguish source-pattern issues from fixture, placement, closure, fabric and import problems before changing the draft. Retry recoverable setup problems. Keep the screenshots and project as evidence of the observed result. A successful numerical solver return, a correct 2D outline, or an off-body rehearsal alone cannot establish on-avatar fit.

[CLO Python API](https://developer.clo3d.com/list.html) for release-specific functions. For map and measurement acquisition, see the [native CLO procedure](../runtime-support.md#插件操作参考).
