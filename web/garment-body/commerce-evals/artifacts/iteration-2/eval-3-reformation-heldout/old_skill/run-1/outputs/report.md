# Reformation original-photo conditional avatar

Actual original-photo Image Gen pose guide and real anatomical mesh fit were executed. This is a single-view conditional M170 avatar, not measured anatomy of the retailer model. No private grader or other run output was read or executed.

## Evidence

- Original: `original.jpg`, 800×1067, SHA256 `6df3525e4024daf7a96a73461c595a7a65b79fa22d5d2a58acd1f2dcc4f41f05`. Source is exact official CDN response, not master resolution. `source.json` preserves retailer claims: product-page model 180.34 cm, size 2, 33/25/34.5 inch bust/waist/hip claims may be generic to product family. These claims were **not** substituted for the authorized M170 prior.
- Actual Image Gen: `images/pose.png`, native 1086×1449. Native tool arguments/output hint retained in `images/native-receipt.json`. One call, no collage or derivative six-image claim. Tool image is illustrative anatomy; hidden knees and pelvis are generated hypotheses.
- Registration: `guide-registration.json`: ORB/RANSAC generated-to-original similarity; 290 inliers, mean inlier error 0.995px. `guide-points.json` records digitised native and original coordinates. Hidden pelvis/knee points were excluded. Approximate visible shoulder/elbow/wrist markers received anatomy checks against original; neck-base guide is not the dress neckline.
- Actual mesh: `neutral.obj`, `posed.obj`, `final-state.json`, full 163 bones, 13380 surface vertices, 26756 triangles. CC0 MakeHuman anatomical asset with actual morph calibration, FK and LBS. 12 cm dimensions and all joint rotations remain editable.
- Same-state images: `photo-render.png` and `views/front.png`, `views/side.png`, `views/back.png`. `views/view-provenance.json` binds images to one state and mesh. Camera is separate, exact source size, explicit orthographic 3×4 world-to-pixel matrix and six fitted parameters. No image stretching.
- `rawdepth.npy`, `depth-valid.png`, `depth-semantics.json`: actual triangle z-buffer, rotated camera-axis Z in conditional cm, larger nearer; invalid background -Infinity. This is avatar geometry, not inferred photographed-person depth.
- `projected-pose.json` contains actual complete world joints/pixels; occlusion not classified. `reference-mask.png` uses GrabCut and saturation/dark foreground cleanup and contour filling, not a learned body detector. It includes garment/hair/clutch/shoes and has no naked-body silhouette truth.

## Fitting and practical limits

Independent `fit.py` starts at provided neutral M170 state. Three bounded blocks optimize camera, then pose/camera, then supported shoulder/arm-length parameters with priors. `parameter-block-history.json` preserves all initial/final blocks and actual objective values. Circumference priors stay 90/72/96 cm, standing height 170 cm; fitted shoulder/arm lengths are conditional mesh parameters. Final dimension/angle values are normalized to 0.1. Head hair and shoe sole are excluded from body-scale claims. Whole-photo initial/final comparisons are retained, as is initial-state-at-final-camera comparison separating camera contribution.

OpenCV whole-envelope IoU initial 0.4974, final 0.6400; contour P95 initial 126.28px, final 102.32px. Full metrics in `metrics.json`; these are garment-envelope diagnostics, not a claim of naked-body accuracy or a benchmark grade. Clothing width and hair dominate mismatch; centre skirt has no naked-leg evidence. There was no independent pre-frozen critical-region protocol in this run, so no assertion that every region passes.

Source-view arms are lowered and approximate visible wrists. Wrist/hand depth, palm orientation and finger flexion remain weakly constrained; clutch contact/grasp is not reconstructed or certified. Side and back expose prior-dependent depth and limb angles and are not independent observed views. Rendered side shows an appreciable prior-dependent sagittal posture and lower-limb separation; do not treat this as recovered true stance. Complete fingers remain neutral prior; a faithful clutch grip is unverified. Independent severe-skin-overlap/collision coverage was NOT_RUN; LBS does not simulate tissue or cloth. No claim of physical fit or complete 3D action validation.

`measurements.json`/CSV separate `prior`, conditional `mesh_parameter` and `unavailable`; anatomical definitions are mesh surface tape paths/landmark distances. Null fields include underbust, bust-point spacing, shoulder slope, waist lengths and crotch arc. Published model dimensions remain provenance, not truth. `neutral-independent-check.json` reconstructs actual asset morphs independently; `artifact-replay-check.json` checks saved-state OBJ and source camera replay. Those are numerical reconstruction checks only.
