# Everlane single-photo conditional avatar

Actual outputs are in `images/` (six independently generated native images), `views/` (same-state real anatomical front/side/back), `photo-render.png`, `final-overlay.png`, and `final-state.json`. `viewer.html` links to the repository Human Studio centimetre/joint editor with the final state preselected; serve the repository root over HTTP. Full editor interaction was NOT_RUN in this evaluation.

The original is the unmodified 2400×3000 Everlane JPEG, SHA-256 eee08dde62e50376cc25fb2b46a59e8a83e5adf5a3f711d75743b0465e945897. Visible details: wide shallow boatneck, white lightweight cotton poplin, concealed front placket, fitted waist darts, long sleeves gathered above broad cuffs, black trousers. Original crop excludes lower legs/feet. Model height and worn size are unavailable.

Mode is `prior_avatar`: shared authorized height170/bust90/waist72/hips96/shoulders37 cm M prior, not recovered person measurements. All12 dimensions remain the initial conditional prior because clothing obscures girths and no absolute scale was observed. Complete163 local bone rotations and calibrated morph weights are saved. OBJ is actual13,380-vertex,26,756-triangle MakeHuman surface in cm, Y-up; FK and LBS are actually executed by the allowed generic runtime. Side/back images and lower-body clothing are inferred hypotheses.

One preliminary Image Gen guide was actually generated before fitting. SIFT/RANSAC registration to the original has77 inliers from88 matches; registration transform and error are saved. Guide neck and hip dots sit on clothing neckline/waistband, so they were rejected as joint truth. Manual approximate internal joint hypotheses were authored directly on the unmodified original and are recorded in reference/landmarks.json.

Own SciPy least-squares fitter ran two genuine front/15-degree-oblique initializations for camera plus shoulder/elbow/head/spine joint blocks. Real side inspection revealed arms far behind the torso; a third bounded depth-regularized block supplied the retained candidate. The first two blocks were reproduced once while extending the executable entry; about30seconds of numerical fitting across both executions, not a10-minute search. State parameters, alternatives and actual evaluations are retained in parameter-history.json and candidate files. This is pose/camera fitting with fixed conditional shape, not a claim to recover true body dimensions. Joint depth regularization is a prior. No hand-contact gesture is present.

Final source camera is an explicit native2400×3000 oblique orthographic projection, including3×4 world-to-pixel matrix, scale/center/yaw/pitch/roll. No PNG stretching. Initial and final masks use the same final camera. GrabCut was executed at600×750 then nearest-resized to source resolution; hair, clothing and imperfect arm gaps limit reference precision. Frozen same-camera whole-image IoU0.6310→0.8431, Dice0.7738→0.9149, symmetric contour mean167.62→82.07px, P95473→266.12px. These compare clothed/hair silhouette to a bare model and do not measure body accuracy. Region metrics are separately saved; no overall visual acceptance is claimed.

Remaining visible geometry limitations: torso/waist and hand silhouettes disagree with garment envelope; shoulder and hip joint locations are inferred; side view retains a pronounced lumbar/torso shape and arm/hand depth ambiguity. Lower legs and hidden anatomy are prior. No tissue, collision, physical cloth fit or true side/back validation was run. Broad garment silhouette agreement does not certify anatomical pose correctness.

Actual pose projections are in projected-pose.json and geometric-pose.png; actual triangle Z-buffer is rawdepth.npy with depth-valid.png and geometric-depth.png. Depth is rotated world Z in cm, larger nearer, invalid background negative infinity. Generated images/pose.png is illustrative and places some points on clothing rather than internal joints. Generated images/depth.png is mostly a grayscale photograph; it is an illustrative style result and is NOT a quantitative depth map. The actual geometric evidence is separate.

Unknown tool timing/token telemetry is null. No private grader was read or invoked.
