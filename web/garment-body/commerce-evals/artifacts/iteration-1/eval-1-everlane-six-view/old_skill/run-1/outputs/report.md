# Everlane single-photo conditional avatar and six views

Executed using frozen skill-v0, original 2400×3000 JPEG, and shared neutral M170 state. No observed second view, no measured model height or clothing size. Mode is `prior_avatar`: all centimetres describe a conditional editable avatar, not measured anatomy of the woman.

Six native independent Image Gen finals are in `images/`: front, side, back, closeup, pose, depth. One preliminary Image Gen pose guide is in `reference/pose-guide.png`. Each final used original plus an actual inspected fitted mesh render; receipts preserve complete prompts, references, hashes and native dimensions. The sheet in `evidence/` is only an inspection contact sheet; every final is the complete native tool output. Full-body legs, shoes, side and back construction are inferred. Generated pose/depth are explicitly illustrative, never detector/Z-buffer measurements.

`reproduce.py` actually ran generic calibrated MakeHuman FK/LBS with two bounded numerical blocks: joint angles and six camera variables; then conditional shoulder/limb lengths plus refined joint/camera. Numerical fitting took about 10 seconds per reproducibility execution. No other fitted states were read. ORB/RANSAC registered the preliminary generated guide to original (157 inliers, mean 2.28 original pixels); guide skeleton has schematic shoulder/pelvis locations and a neckline neck marker, so anatomical targets are explicitly manually corrected and weighted, with pelvis low weight. There is no automatic pose detector. Fitting objective uses inferred joint residuals and priors; the separate silhouette metrics are validation, not an optimized bare-body truth.

State is full 163 bones, actual anatomical 13,380 surface vertices and 26,756 triangles. Height/bust/waist/hips remain generic priors 170/90/72/96 cm. Conditional shoulder width 36.8 cm and upper arm 29.1 cm; forearm remains 23 cm. JSON, neutral/posed OBJ cm, source-sized camera matrix, projected actual joints, raw rendered axial depth and validity mask are saved. Canonical `views/` were saved with Runtime.save, using exactly the same shape and full pose. The optional `viewer.html` embeds actual Human Studio, loads this fitted state and exposes its real dimension and bone controls; serve repository root over HTTP. Browser interactions were not exercised during this run.

State export initially failed independent checker because solver floating-point angles were not on Human Studio's 0.1-degree grid. Failure is preserved in `evidence/neutral-independent.json`; export now normalizes angles/dimensions before recalibration and rerendering. `evidence/neutral-independent-final.json` independently reconstructs neutral mesh from asset/morph coefficients and measures the named paths: maximum target residual 0.000041 cm. This is model calibration precision, not photographic measurement accuracy. Image Gen references preserve the almost-identical pre-quantization state/renders under `reference/generation-*`; their actual hashes are retained in receipts.

Frozen-final-camera original comparison: IoU 0.631 → 0.801, Dice 0.774 → 0.890; symmetric contour mean 169.0 → 96.4 original pixels, P95 492.0 → 326.3. Reference segmentation was GrabCut at 640×800, then nearest-neighbor upsampled; it includes hair and clothes and has a visible erroneous background patch to the right of the hair. Do not read these numbers as true naked-body silhouette accuracy. Native 2400×3000 images were rasterized directly without stretching.

Visual inspection: the final projected shoulders/arms approximately follow the photo, but shoulders remain somewhat wide and head surface excludes hair. Canonical side view exposes a substantial torso arch and arm depth is uncertain; fingers remain neutral rather than reconstructed. This is the weakest 3D view. Neutral fingers splay more than the photograph. These limitations prevent a claim of fully recovered 3D anatomy or exact gesture. Hidden lower body remains neutral prior. No physical cloth/collision simulation was run.

Generated image inspection: boatneck, concealed placket, waist shaping, white cotton, pushed long sleeves and black trousers generally remain; exact sleeve folds and neckline differ and front/pose/depth framing is not perfectly matched. Generated back darts and lower legs are hypotheses. The depth-style final is largely photographic grayscale rather than strictly monotonic geometric depth. Real geometric depth is separately `rawdepth.npy` (rotated world Z, cm, larger toward camera; invalid -inf) and `depth-valid.png`. Generated finals are not registered pixelwise to geometry.

Measurements with unresolved anatomy are null. Token counts and per-image latency are unavailable and saved null. No private grader, self-grade, sibling output, holdout, alternate skill or prior photo fitting solution was used. No canonical skill, shared runtime, evaluation HTML, or repository commit was changed by this run.
